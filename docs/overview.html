Designed for the constraints

Ordinary business software breaks here. This was built for it.

Three realities of trading in Zimbabwe shaped every architectural decision — they aren't edge cases, they're the design brief.

01 — INFRASTRUCTURE

The power and the line drop out

Grid and mobile data are intermittent. A cloud-only till that freezes when the connection dies is useless at the counter.

→ Offline-first, syncs on reconnect
02 — CURRENCY

The money won't sit still

ZiG and USD circulate together at rates that move week to week. Store only a currency label and last month's takings become meaningless.

→ Transaction-time rate capture
03 — PAYMENTS

Value moves by phone, not card

EcoCash, OneMoney, ZIPIT and PayNow do the work that card rails do elsewhere — including split payments across two currencies.

→ Mobile-money-native settlement
The engineering that matters

Three decisions do the heavy lifting.

Not a feature list — the design choices that make the rest trustworthy.

CONTRIBUTION Ⅰ

Money keeps its meaning

Every monetary event stores amount, currency, base currency and the exchange rate as at that moment — exact decimals, never floats. A sale made at yesterday's rate stays true after any devaluation.

Transaction-time multi-currency
CONTRIBUTION Ⅱ

A sale is never lost or double-counted

Sales and stock moves are immutable, client-keyed events. Retries de-duplicate; two offline tills selling the last unit are both kept and flagged. Stock-on-hand is a fold over the ledger, so it can't drift.

Offline-first event sync
CONTRIBUTION Ⅲ

AI that answers from your own books

Ask “why did profit fall last month?” and the assistant retrieves your real figures first, then explains them — grounded, tenant-isolated, with an evidence trail. It never invents a number.

Retrieval-grounded assistant
One login, three workspaces

The system shows each person only their job.

Role-based access decides the landing page and the powers — enforced in the token, again at the API, and once more by database row-level security.

Owner
Full access · lands on finance
  • Finance & inventory per branch and combined
  • Open & close branches, add or remove staff
  • Live till monitor — every sale as it happens
  • Correct recorded payments; full audit trail
  • Forecasts, anomalies & the AI assistant
Manager
Staff & stock · lands on team
  • Hire & remove staff — own confidential password
  • Assign branches, clock people on / off duty
  • Update goods prices and manage inventory
  • Handle online orders; live monitor
  • No branch creation, no owner financials
Till operator
The counter · lands on POS
  • POS with an editable cart & quantity picker
  • Camera / scanner barcode add-to-cart
  • Split-currency & mobile-money payments
  • Printed receipts; fulfil online orders
  • Sees only its own sales — no reports
