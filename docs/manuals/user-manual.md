# SIMS AI — User Manual

*For shop owners, managers and cashiers. No accounting or IT knowledge needed.*

## 1. Getting started

1. Open SIMS AI in your browser (or the Android app).
2. First time? Choose **Register your business**, enter your business name,
   your name, email and a password (8+ characters). Your business, main shop
   and owner account are created together.
3. Returning? Sign in with your email and password (and your 2FA code if you
   enabled it).

## 2. Setting up your products (Inventory)

1. Go to **Inventory**.
2. Fill in the product name, selling price (what the customer pays — VAT
   included), cost price (what you pay your supplier) and opening quantity.
3. Press **Add product**. A barcode (EAN-13) is generated automatically; you
   can print it or use your supplier's existing barcode instead.
4. The **Stock levels** table shows what's on hand. Anything at or below its
   reorder level is flagged **⚠ Reorder**, and the **AI reorder suggestions**
   card tells you how much to order based on how fast each item sells.

## 3. Selling (Point of Sale)

1. Go to **Point of Sale**.
2. Tap products to add them to the cart (or scan/type a barcode in the
   search box).
3. Choose how the customer pays — cash, EcoCash, OneMoney, ZIPIT, PayNow or
   bank — and press **Charge**.
4. The sale is saved instantly, VAT is worked out for you, and you can
   download or print the **PDF receipt**.
5. **No network? Keep selling.** On the mobile app every sale is stored on
   the phone and sent to the server automatically when the network returns.
   Nothing is ever lost or counted twice.

## 4. Understanding your Dashboard

- **Net revenue / Net profit** tiles show this period's performance with a
  small arrow comparing it to the previous period.
- Use the range buttons (7 / 30 / 90 days, month-to-date) to change the
  period.
- The **revenue chart** shows daily takings plus a 7-day forecast (dashed)
  with a confidence band.
- **Money in, by payment method** shows how much arrived as cash vs EcoCash
  vs bank — useful for reconciling your float.
- **Alerts & anomalies** flags unusual things: a day with strange revenue,
  items sold far below list price, frequent voids, or oversells that
  happened while tills were offline.

## 5. Sales history

The **Sales** page lists every receipt with date, payment method, total and
status. From here you can re-download any receipt or **void** a sale (with
confirmation) — voiding puts the stock back and keeps the original record
for the audit trail.

## 6. Asking the AI Assistant

Go to **AI Assistant** and ask in plain language:

- *"How is my profit this month?"*
- *"What should I reorder?"*
- *"Forecast my revenue for next week."*
- *"Any suspicious activity?"*

Every answer is calculated from **your own records** — the assistant never
guesses numbers.

## 7. Working in two currencies

Prices can be in USD or ZWG. When you record today's ZWG rate (ask your
manager/accountant, or it comes from the RBZ feed), every sale keeps the
rate that applied at the moment of sale — so last month's takings stay
correct even after the rate changes.

## 8. Protecting your account

- Turn on **two-factor authentication** (Settings → 2FA): scan the code
  with Google Authenticator or similar; from then on sign-in needs your
  password plus the 6-digit code.
- Never share your password. Each staff member should have their own
  account with the right role (see the Administrator Manual).
