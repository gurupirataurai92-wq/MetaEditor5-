What the numbers mean

TermWhat it means for you
Auction gradeThe Japanese inspector's overall score. 5 is near-new, 4.5 and 4 are clean used units, 3.5 shows honest wear and normally the best value.
CIF priceCost of the vehicle, insurance and freight to your port. Import duty, VAT and port charges are separate and quoted per country.
In transitAlready paid for and on the water. You can reserve it before it lands.
To orderNot yet purchased. We bid for it at auction on your behalf against an agreed maximum.
RHD / LHDRight- or left-hand drive. Check your country's registration rules before ordering.
